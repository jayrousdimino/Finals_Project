Waste Categories

Know your waste. Segregate it right.

🍃

Biodegradable Waste

Organic materials that can break down naturally, such as fruit and vegetable scraps, leaves, and garden waste. These may be suitable for composting.

♻️

Recyclable Waste

Materials such as paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass, and some plastics. Check local recycling rules because accepted materials vary by facility.

🗑️

Residual Waste

Waste that cannot be reused, composted, or accepted for recycling locally. Follow your community's collection and disposal rules.

⚠️

Hazardous Waste

Items such as batteries, fluorescent lamps, chemicals, and some electronic waste that require special handling. Do not mix them with ordinary household waste.
