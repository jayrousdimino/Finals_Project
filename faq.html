Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common waste-management questions.

Why should I separate waste?

Segregation keeps recyclable and compostable materials from being mixed with other waste and supports safer handling.

Do all plastics go in the recycling bin?

No. Accepted plastics vary by local program. Check the recycling rules in your area before placing plastics in a collection bin.

Should recyclable containers be clean?

Empty and rinse containers when appropriate, then let them dry. Follow the instructions of your local recycling facility.

Where should I bring batteries and chemicals?

Do not mix them with ordinary household waste. Contact your local government or an authorized collection program for instructions.

Are the locations on this website verified?

No. The current facility entries are sample placeholders. Confirm addresses, accepted materials, schedules, and operating hours with the facility or local government.
