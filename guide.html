Recycling Guide

Follow these simple steps for proper waste segregation and recycling.

🗑️
1

Segregate

Separate biodegradable, recyclable, residual, and hazardous waste.

🚿
2

Clean

Empty and clean accepted recyclable containers when appropriate.

📦
3

Store

Keep recyclable materials sorted and dry until collection or drop-off.

♻️
4

Recycle

Bring materials to a facility or collection program that accepts them.

🌱 Tip: Small steps, big impact. Start today for a better tomorrow!
