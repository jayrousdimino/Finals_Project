Simple Eco-Friendly Tips

Small everyday habits can help reduce waste and protect the environment.

🛍️

Bring Reusable Bags

Keep reusable bags handy to reduce the need for single-use shopping bags.

🚰

Use Refillable Bottles

Choose a reusable water bottle and refill it when safe drinking water is available.

🍌

Compost Organics

Where facilities and local rules allow, compost fruit and vegetable scraps.

🔧

Repair and Reuse

Repair useful items and donate or repurpose things that are still in good condition.

🧴

Check Packaging

Look for recycling labels and confirm what your local collection program accepts.

🔋

Handle Special Waste Safely

Keep batteries, lamps, and chemicals separate and ask your local government about proper collection.
